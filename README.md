###

<h2>🚀 About Me</h2>

<p>I started my career in <strong>telecommunications</strong> — radio links, ISP infrastructure, routers, firewalls. After more than four years troubleshooting connectivity for companies and individuals, I realised what I really enjoyed wasn't fixing things. It was understanding <em>why</em> they broke.</p>

<p>That curiosity led me to software. Today I build backend systems and I'm focused on <strong>Rust</strong>, not because it's trendy, but because it forces me to understand what's actually happening under the hood — memory, types, errors, the things that languages like Python politely hide.</p>
